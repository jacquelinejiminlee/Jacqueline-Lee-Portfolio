01 — OVERVIEW

Designing a contemporary bag line rooted in Seoul craft.

Avécbag is a contemporary bag line from the Seoul-based studio nuée, built on a "Made in Korea" manufacturing philosophy and a focus on versatile, everyday design. As an in-house product designer, I worked across the full product lifecycle — from concept and material selection to final production and campaign rollout.

My work ranged from developing standalone product families (rope straps, gymsacks, totes) to designing brand collaborations that extended Avécbag's identity into fashion trade shows, sportswear partnerships, and socially driven editorial campaigns.

02 — PRODUCT

Rope Strap — color as identity.

A modular rope strap system designed to transform Avécbag's core silhouettes. Built from polyester and nickel hardware at 98cm, each colorway — red, purple, yellow — carries a different energy while sharing the same engineered twist pattern. The straps were designed to work as swappable accessories across the existing bag line.

Rope Strap Collection · 98cm · Polyester, Nickel
03 — PRODUCT

Gymsack — neon utility.

A three-colorway gymsack line that brought a sportier, more active-wear direction into the Avécbag catalog. Neon pink and neon orange reference athletic safety gear, while the black colorway uses a neon yellow contrast strap for the same visual pop in a more versatile base. Each piece keeps the signature nuée label centered on the front panel.

Gymsack Collection · Neon Pink · Neon Orange · Black
04 — COLLABORATION

"This is not just black" — a tote for World Sight Day.

Designed in collaboration with the Korean Braille Library and calligraphy artist Hwang Jun-young, this tote was released on October 15 — 흰지팡이의날 (White Cane Day / World Sight Day) — to recognize the rights and visibility of the visually impaired community.

The removable embroidered patch reads "This is not just black" — a statement that reframes how we understand vision and presence. The piece was produced alongside a sculptural work ("꽃", 2023) that extended the concept into fine art. The patch attaches with a bright green safety pin, making the act of taking it on and off part of the product's meaning.

nuée × Korean Braille Library × Hwang Jun-young · October 2023
05 — COLLABORATION

nuée × ASICS — a booth, a mascot, a narrative.

For the "Feel the next generation of cushioning" exhibition, nuée partnered with ASICS to design a full booth experience built around custom rabbit mascots dressed in capes, chain collars, and sneakers. The characters functioned as brand ambassadors across the space, placed on acrylic plinths above the signature ASICS logo floor graphics.

My role focused on the nuée-side product and prop design — giving the sportswear partnership a distinctly nuée voice while fitting inside ASICS' retail footprint.

nuée × ASICS · Exhibition Booth · Feel the next generation of cushioning
06 — COLLABORATION

SEESAW × SHOW — a bag as editorial object.

A collaboration lookbook and packaging system developed with SEESAW, presented as a cohesive editorial magazine on vivid orange stock. The spreads pair models wearing the SHOW bag line in monochrome photography, creating a high-contrast visual rhythm that let the product sit as the subject of each page.

Beyond the editorial, the collaboration extended into hang tag design, business card systems, and custom packaging — each surface treated as a continuation of the same visual language.

SEESAW × SHOW · Editorial, Packaging, Collateral · Designed by Jihoon & Jimin Lee
07 — CAMPAIGN

COTERIE New York — taking Avécbag global.

Campaign visuals developed for Avécbag's presentation at COTERIE New York (2.18–20), one of the largest fashion trade shows in North America. The lookbook brought together studio photography and clean display compositions, positioning the bags against stairwells, pedestals, and styled denim lifestyle moments.

The campaign tagline — "Easy match, easy carry, but stylish" — captured what Avécbag was building toward: versatile product design made for everyday use, finished to the standard of a contemporary fashion brand.
