Problem

Everything looked like GSSC

GSSF runs four programs, but GSSC, the competition, was the name people knew. Every program used its blue and logo, so Media and AI Labs looked like part of the competition instead of the foundation.

Brand foundations

Brand architecture

Before designing anything, I set up how the four programs relate. GSSF is the nonprofit that runs everything, so it became the parent brand.

Logo

Logo family

All four logos share the same stacked letters. The box is what changes: it marks everything that belongs to the foundation.

GSSF sealThe square works like a seal: steady and official. Anything inside it belongs to GSSF.

GSSC monogramIt's the competition, not a foundation program, so I left it open. That also kept the monogram people already recognized.
