01 / CONTEXT

A ready-to-eat chicken brand moving from DTC to retail.

Im'peccable Chicken came to AMA USC preparing for a Shark Tank pitch and their first retail rollout. The existing packaging wasn't built for either context.

The goal was a visual system that could scale across flavors, work on a grocery shelf, and translate to both a pitch deck and a customer's inbox.

02 / COLOR & IDENTITY

Color exploration.

Tested four directions against the original palette: a navy-grounded system, a warm neutral with orange accents, and two saturated directions anchored in the existing orange.

COLOR PALETTE EXPLORATION · FOUR DIRECTIONS
03 / PACKAGING

Packaging system.

Ten rounds of iteration to land on the final direction: a stenciled wordmark, hero product photography, and color-coded flavor fields.

Three flavors — Orange Habanero, Classic Teriyaki, Original Pepper — each with its own gradient anchor and a consistent callout system for protein, calories, and tagline. The back panel covers brand voice, when to eat, and how to eat.

04 / EMAIL FLOWS

Email lifecycle system.

A twelve-email sequence covering welcome, abandoned cart, win-back, and brand story. Built in Klaviyo, using the same color and type system as the packaging.

Each email is structured around one CTA and kept to three sections or fewer.

Social, Shark Tank, and brand world.

The brand system extended into static social posts, logo exploration, and the Shark Tank pitch deck — including the set design concept for the on-stage presentation.
