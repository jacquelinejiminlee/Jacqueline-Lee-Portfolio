Jacqueline Lee — Design

I'm a designer who tends to ask "why?" before "how?" I started in design because I loved solving problems creatively, but over time I realized I was often more curious about the decisions behind the design: who are we trying to reach, what problem are they actually facing, why is this the right message.

Working on marketing for AI products pushed that further — I'm most excited by identifying pain points, defining how a product solves them, and translating that value into messaging and creative that makes the solution immediately clear. Design is still core to how I think and communicate, but my interests are expanding upstream, toward customer insight, positioning, messaging, and product marketing.
